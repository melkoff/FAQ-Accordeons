Getting here

How to find us

Coming from a nearby city? Select your location for directions to our Guelph clinic.

Drive time: approx. 50 min
  • Head west on the QEW toward Hamilton
  • Take the Highway 6 North exit - this road leads directly to Guelph
  • As you enter Guelph, Highway 6 becomes Hanlon Expressway
  • Exit at Stone Road and head east
  • Turn left onto Westmount Road North
  • Look for number 77 on your right, Suite 201
Arrive at 77 Westmount Road, Suite 201, Guelph
